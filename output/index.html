Put Your Phone Away!

Next time you are commuting or waiting in line, I want you to put your phone in your pocket and just be there. You can find many videos on YouTube which highlight the benefits of being bored. Many of my greatest ideas came to me whilst I was just thinking about things. Think about your shower thoughts but even more powerful!

I would even go a step further and say to avoid listening to music when you can too. Think about how you would feel about your favourite song if you kept listening to it on repeat, a similar thing will happen if you generally listen to music non stop. Experiment with not listening to any music for a while and then come back to it and you will feel a much stronger connection with all of your favourite songs.

Recently I went to a concert and I didn't take any videos of the concert. One thing I noticed is that it was easier to enjoy the really great parts because I wasn't worried about capturing the "best" moments and worrying if maybe this moment was even better than the previous and therefore needed to be videod.

Though in general I think it's just sad to look around and seeing everyone with their heads down and swiping, especially because when I look at their screen they're never doing anything urgently, usually it's just scrolling of some form. Embrace the boredom! It won't stick around for long and your mind will be wandering in no time.

New Years Resolutions

Everyone does them, but no one does them! I think they're still fun and worth doing, and with some help I think you can also do them!

I do think the types of new years resolutions you make are important, and I think you should know how to make good new years resolutions! New years resolutions are quite sneaky because they are just regular old goals, which masquarade as events.

So if new years resolutions are just goals, how do you make a good goal? So of course we first make sure it is a SMART goal: it should be specific, measurable, attainable, relevant, and time bound. Luckily we get time bound for free because we have at most one year to do it! Specifically I think many new year resolutions are either unattainable or not relevant. For example I've heard of people wanting to fully cut out sugar or going running every day. Do you really want that‽
