Everyone does them, but no one does them! I think they're still fun and worth doing, and with some help I think you can also do them!

I do think the types of new years resolutions you make are important, and I think you should know how to make good new years resolutions! New years resolutions are quite sneaky because they are just regular old goals, which masquarade as events.

So if new years resolutions are just goals, how do you make a good goal? So of course we first make sure it is a SMART goal: it should be specific, measurable, attainable, relevant, and time bound. Luckily we get time bound for free because we have at most one year to do it! Specifically I think many new year resolutions are either unattainable or not relevant. For example I've heard of people wanting to fully cut out sugar or going running every day. Do you really want that‽

You can look at some statistics and you will see that on average your new years resolution will fail around January 12 or something. But wait! What does it even mean to fail? Why does 'failing' a goal mean you need to stop pursuing whatever reason you set the goal in the first place? You can just keep going! It's important to acknowledge that when you start out wanting to make a big new change in your life, you will fail! Or at least you will have days or weeks where you fail, once you acknowledge this, you will find it is easier to get yourself back on the horse after falling off. This is the first step to maintaing your new goal.

Next you need to take your goal, and you need to make it simpler. Got that? Okay, now I need you to go ahead and make it even simpler. Don't start your 6 day per week gym plan just yet, just go 2 or 3 days a week and get a good 30-45 minute workout in. Being able to show a streak of consistency of doing simpler things really adds up so that over a longer period of time it becomes more of a habit and scaling up is a lot easier.
