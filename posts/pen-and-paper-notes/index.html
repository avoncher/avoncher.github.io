Pen And Paper Notes

Last edited 7 November 2026.

Either my smartest or my dumbest idea!

I'd really like to try next semester is trying to do as much of my work with a physical note book as I can. There are some benefits of this (in theory). It should be far harder to get distracted when you don't have the whole internet at your fingertips! There are of course the studies that show memory is better when handwriting instead of typing. It becomes harder to use language models in studying or doing work, and I believe there are studies showing that end result learning is better when you do not use these models.

Though I need to weigh how strict I will be with this. For example will I watch lectures on my laptop at times and then use a notebook, what about textbooks? And what about when I need to look something up? Will I let myself use the AI when looking stuff up? I don't know!

I think the biggest change will be being physically present in the lecture theaters taking physical notes without a device to conveniently distract me. I'll figure out the finer details when the time comes, but I think with things like this its important to not get lost in the purity or the aesthetics of the idea, but rather simply acknowledge the idea is just a possible path to get to a desired end point.
